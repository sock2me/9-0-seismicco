## Visual Theme
Heavyweight industrial precision with high-contrast safety cues. Deep charcoal-black foundational surfaces paired with crisp engineering white, structured slate borders, and high-visibility safety crimson red accents.

## Art Direction
- Concept: Heavy-duty seismic restraint engineering built for high-consequence environments.
- Layout rhythm: Structured, technical, and high-density industrial grid.

## Components
- Motif: Precision steel hardware plates, crisp 1px structural borders, sharp industrial geometry (rounded-none to rounded-sm), high-contrast data callouts.
- Buttons: Solid bevel-free mechanical buttons with bold weight (rounded-sm), heavy primary crimson for immediate action and dark steel for secondary specs.
- Containers: Solid background panels with sharp borders (`border border-border` or `border-dark-border`), micro-inset dividers, and clear technical separation.
- Accent architecture: Crimson accent used as active status highlights, load indicators, and primary action anchors.
- Sections: Alternating between clean clinical light surfaces and dark heavy-load engineering bands (`py-16 md:py-24`).
- Imagery: High-resolution photography of industrial brackets, cleanroom equipment anchoring, laboratory containment, and structural steel connections.

## Do's and Don'ts
- DO maintain razor-sharp technical contrast and precise grid containment.
- DO highlight real-world applications in hospitals, mechanical rooms, and labs.
- DON'T use soft floating pastel shadows or vague consumer-tech styling.
- DON'T invent fake numerical percentage counters or unverified regulatory claim codes.
